HOW TO FIX AURORA STORE ON YOUR PORTAL
======================================

This folder fixes Aurora Store so it can install apps on your Portal.
You do NOT need to type any commands. Just follow these steps:

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere on your PC (like your Desktop).

2. Plug your Portal into your PC with a USB cable.
   - Use a DATA cable. Some cheap cables only charge and will NOT work.
   - Make sure USB debugging (ADB) is turned ON on your Portal.

3. On your PORTAL screen, a message may pop up asking to allow
   USB debugging. Tap ALLOW.

4. On your PC, double-click: DOUBLE-CLICK-ME-TO-FIX.bat

5. Follow what it says. It will tell you when it worked.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- I waited 30 seconds but never saw your Portal
  -> Try a different USB cable. This fixes it 9 times out of 10.
  -> Make sure USB debugging is actually turned on in the Portal settings.
- It says Aurora Store is not installed
  -> Install Aurora Store on your Portal first, then run the fix again.
- Something went wrong
  -> Take a screenshot of the black window and send it to the person
     who gave you this folder.
